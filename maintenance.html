AeroNest Terms of Flight & Operations
1. FAA Part 107 & Airspace Compliance

All flight operations are conducted strictly in compliance with applicable civil aviation authorities, including FAA Part 107 and DGCA guidelines. Flights in controlled airspace require prior LAANC digital authorization or airspace waivers.

2. Weather & Safety Grounding

The Pilot in Command (PIC) reserves final authority to postpone, reschedule, or cancel any flight if weather conditions (gusts exceeding 25 knots, precipitation, or low visibility) compromise flight safety. In such cases, deposits transfer to the next available flight window without penalty.

3. Copyright & Commercial Usage

Clients receive full commercial usage rights for delivered media as specified in the service package. Unedited RAW files and proprietary flight telemetry logs remain the property of AeroNest unless licensed separately.

4. Turnaround & Revisions

Standard delivery timelines are 24-48 hours for photography and 3-5 business days for edited cinema reels. Two rounds of color grade/edit adjustments are included in all packages.

AeroNest Privacy & Data Policy
1. Aerial Privacy Guidelines

AeroNest operates under strict aerial privacy protocols. We do not deliberately capture, catalog, or publish recognizable imagery of private non-client properties, residents, or restricted industrial installations without explicit written authorization.

2. Information Collection

When you request a flight quote or book an aerial shoot, we collect flight coordinates, property addresses, client contact details, and production briefs solely for flight planning and FAA compliance filings.

3. Media Storage & Encryption

Deliverables are securely hosted on encrypted cloud vaults for 90 days following project completion. We do not sell or share your contact data with third-party advertisers.

4. Cookies & Newsletter

We use lightweight local storage to remember your theme and language preferences. You may unsubscribe from our monthly flight digest at any time with one click.
