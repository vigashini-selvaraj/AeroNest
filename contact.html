AERONEST POLICIES
Terms & Flight Policy
1. Part 107 Commercial Flight Compliance

All AeroNest operations comply strictly with FAA Part 107 (and DGCA / national civil aviation authorities). Our pilots hold commercial drone licenses and full airspace risk authorizations.

2. Weather & Safety Ground Rules

Safety is non-negotiable. Flights will be rescheduled at zero cost to the client in the event of rain, sustained winds exceeding 20 knots, restricted temporary flight limits (TFRs), or severe geomagnetic interference.

3. Commercial Licensing & Usage Rights

Clients receive full commercial usage rights for delivered media as specified in the service package. Unedited RAW files and proprietary flight telemetry logs remain the property of AeroNest unless licensed separately.

4. Turnaround & Revisions

Standard delivery timelines are 24-48 hours for photography and 3-5 business days for edited cinema reels. Two rounds of color grade/edit adjustments are included in all packages.

PRIVACY & DATA SECURITY
Privacy Policy
1. Information We Collect

We collect project details, contact information, GPS flight coordinates, and shoot requirements submitted via our inquiry forms to coordinate flight plans and deliver high-resolution assets.

2. Airspace & Property Privacy Safeguards

AeroNest respects private property and personal privacy. During aerial capture, sensors are angled to focus strictly on the commissioned subject, avoiding adjacent residential interiors and restricted neighbor premises.

3. Cloud Storage & Asset Delivery

Deliverables are securely hosted on encrypted cloud vaults for 90 days following project completion. We do not sell or share your contact data with third-party advertisers.

4. Cookies & Newsletter

We use lightweight local storage to remember your theme and language preferences. You may unsubscribe from our monthly flight digest at any time with one click.
